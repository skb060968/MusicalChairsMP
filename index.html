How to Play

  1. 🎯

    Create or join a room using a 4-letter code, then choose a unique avatar. 2 to 8 players.

  2. 🎵

    Listen to the music. Your avatars orbit the chairs for a random 30 to 60 seconds, with no countdown.

  3. 🫳

    Drag when the music stops. Move your avatar toward any free chair.

  4. 🪑

    Enter a chair's capture zone. The first valid claim wins that chair; release is not required.

  5. 💥

    Players without chairs are out. The last player standing wins, and the host can start a rematch.
