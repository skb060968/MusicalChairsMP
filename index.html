🪑 🎵 🪑 MUSICAL CHAIRS

Party elimination · 2–8 players

How to Play

  1. 🎯

    Create or join a room with a 4-letter code and pick an avatar — 2 to 8 players, each avatar unique.

  2. 🎵

    The music plays for 10, 20 or 30 seconds — nobody knows which — while your avatars circle the chairs. There is always one chair fewer than players.

  3. 🫳

    When it stops, grab a chair: drag your avatar onto a free one, or just tap the chair. You have 10 seconds.

  4. 🏆

    Left standing? You're out. Rounds continue until one player remains. The game runs on a shared clock, so it never waits for anyone — not even the host.
